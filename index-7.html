WHO WE ARE

Rooted in community.
Driven by compassion.

Swayamkrushi Rural and Urban Development Society is a non-profit organization established on 13 September 2017.

We work towards the integrated development of underserved communities, especially women and children, across Nellore, Tirupati and Prakasam districts of Andhra Pradesh. Our initiatives focus on dignity, empowerment and self-reliance.

CARE THAT HONOURS A LIFETIME
A PLACE FOR CARE & BELONGING

Growing older should never mean growing alone.

Our old age home support work is guided by a simple belief: every older person deserves safety, respect, companionship and compassionate care.

We aim to create an environment where elders feel heard, valued and connected. Contact our team to learn about current facilities, admissions and ways to support elderly care.
